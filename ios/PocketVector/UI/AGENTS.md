# UI visual ownership

This subtree belongs to the Art Director for appearance, layout, typography,
color, animation, and visual accessibility.

- Preserve existing coordinator actions, domain mutations, navigation routes,
  purchase behavior, and service behavior.
- A requested behavioral change must be handed to Technical or PM rather than
  mixed into an Art commit.
- Verify safe areas at compact and wide landscape sizes, Dynamic Type,
  VoiceOver labels, contrast, reduced motion, and both iPhone and iPad layouts.
- Keep the playfield visible and prevent overlays or chrome from obscuring
  gameplay-critical information.
