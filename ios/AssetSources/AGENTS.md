# Art source ownership

This subtree belongs to the Art Director workstream.

- Preserve editable, high-quality source art separately from shipping output.
- Prefer deterministic regeneration through `ios/Tools` where a tool exists.
- Do not introduce NFL names, logos, uniform designs, or confusingly similar
  trademarks. Real teams are regional mood and palette references only.
- Record source-to-runtime output mappings in the Art Director handoff.
- Do not change runtime code from this subtree's task. Request integration from
  Technical when scale, anchors, frame timing, or rendering behavior must
  change.
